About Aurora

Perched on the 32nd floor of the Aurora Tower, this candle-lit dining room offers a seasonal modern European menu served alongside one of London's most photographed skylines. Chef Lila Moreau's tasting menu changes weekly and the wine list spans 240 references curated by head sommelier Etienne Caron.

Whether you're planning a date night, a milestone celebration or a quiet business dinner, Aurora's intimate booths, soft jazz quartet and golden-hour service create an unmistakably premium atmosphere.
